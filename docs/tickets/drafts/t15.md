## What to build

Inspect and simulate the SW421 screening supply branches down to their LV buses, with the model boundary clearly shown.

Scope: SW421 / Screening.

Implement this as a complete model/session/UI/test slice using the approved simulation-session boundary in the parent specification. Synthetic fixtures must be explicitly identified. Real-network interpretations require source evidence or owner confirmation; unresolved facts remain labelled unconfirmed. Successful software tests do not constitute model acceptance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Visually review the SW421 HV sheet and the downstream incomer/bus portions needed to identify final LV endpoints, including the screening VSD and MCC bus arrangements.
- [ ] Model evidenced source interface, switching/earth devices, transformer windings and bus sections with stable identities and source locations.
- [ ] Show final LV bus boundaries and omit outgoing LV drive/motor circuits even where detailed sheets include their controls or interlocks.
- [ ] Render the screening view with supported switching, source/earth results, impacts, tracing and evidence inspection.
- [ ] Record discrepancies and unresolved physical interlocks with affected controls disabled; retain unaccepted starting states visibly.
- [ ] Update coverage and demonstrate structurally valid, source-referenced area data with session regression checks.

## What to build

Inspect and simulate the connected SW454 and SW505 supply areas through their final LV buses.

Scope: SW454 / SW505 / lump stockpile and wet scrubbing.

Implement this as a complete model/session/UI/test slice using the approved simulation-session boundary in the parent specification. Synthetic fixtures must be explicitly identified. Real-network interpretations require source evidence or owner confirmation; unresolved facts remain labelled unconfirmed. Successful software tests do not constitute model acceptance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Visually review the supplied SW454 and SW505 HV SLDs plus the downstream bus/incomer information needed to locate final LV endpoints.
- [ ] Preserve the main-switchroom interface and the evidenced SW454-to-SW505 relationship, without duplicating equipment represented on several sheets.
- [ ] Represent supported switching/earth devices, bus sections, winding relationships, endpoint buses and evidenced interlock conditions.
- [ ] Render each area's detailed view and demonstrate source/earth status, impacts and tracing across their interface.
- [ ] Record differences, unsupported source/state interpretations and unclear interlocks for the accumulated owner review, with uncertainty/control blocking intact.
- [ ] Update document coverage, validate cross-references and add evidence-based session regression checks without modelling outgoing LV circuits.

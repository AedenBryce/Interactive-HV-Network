## What to build

Inspect and simulate the RM406/RM410 CV006 supply areas through their endpoint buses and documented cross-area interfaces.

Scope: CV006 / RM406 / RM410.

Implement this as a complete model/session/UI/test slice using the approved simulation-session boundary in the parent specification. Synthetic fixtures must be explicitly identified. Real-network interpretations require source evidence or owner confirmation; unresolved facts remain labelled unconfirmed. Successful software tests do not constitute model acceptance.

- [ ] Visually review the supplied CV006 overview, RMU and downstream endpoint SLDs; preserve the overhead-line and hopper-area interface references.
- [ ] Represent evidenced RMU positions, earth connections, transformer relationships and endpoint buses, including downstream HV equipment where encountered.
- [ ] Retain equipment aliases and differences between overview and detailed drawings as evidence-linked review records.
- [ ] Render the areas and demonstrate supported switching, connectivity/impact explanations and cross-area path inspection.
- [ ] Unknown external paths, starting states and interlock conditions stay explicitly unconfirmed; affected unresolved controls remain disabled.
- [ ] Update document coverage and validate the complete area model with evidence-based session regressions.

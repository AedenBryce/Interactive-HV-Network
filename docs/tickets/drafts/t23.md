## What to build

Inspect and simulate the mine/camp network covered by the supplied legacy and distribution drawings through its final LV buses.

Scope: Supplied mine distribution, camp and legacy network sheets.

Implement this as a complete model/session/UI/test slice using the approved simulation-session boundary in the parent specification. Synthetic fixtures must be explicitly identified. Real-network interpretations require source evidence or owner confirmation; unresolved facts remain labelled unconfirmed. Successful software tests do not constitute model acceptance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Visually review the supplied mine/camp distribution, camp switchboard and legacy network/fault-study SLD sheets for relevant topology, sources and endpoint arrangements.
- [ ] Record which older depictions duplicate or differ from other areas without declaring the oldest or newest sheet automatically authoritative.
- [ ] Represent evidenced HV supply paths, source/interface points, equipment states/positions and final LV bus arrangements, excluding outgoing LV circuits and electrical calculations.
- [ ] Preserve unresolved village/mine/external coverage as explicit boundary questions; a missing exact reference filename does not itself prove missing coverage.
- [ ] Render the covered areas with working supported controls, connectivity/impact/path inspection and evidence/review status.
- [ ] Update the source coverage register so every assigned sheet has a disposition; validate the area model and record all unsupported extensions for final owner review.

## What to build

Inspect and simulate RM721 and the supplied services/workshop/conveyor/laboratory branches through their endpoint buses.

Scope: RM721 / RM712 / RM713 / RM714 / RM412 / RM413 / RM413A.

Implement this as a complete model/session/UI/test slice using the approved simulation-session boundary in the parent specification. Synthetic fixtures must be explicitly identified. Real-network interpretations require source evidence or owner confirmation; unresolved facts remain labelled unconfirmed. Successful software tests do not constitute model acceptance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Visually review the supplied services, workshop, dump-pocket and conveyor RMU/SLD sources and their final-bus details.
- [ ] Preserve the main-switchroom and Desands interface identities; do not infer the direction or existence of a cross-area connection merely from proximity on an overview.
- [ ] Represent evidenced incoming, tie, switching/earth, transformer and final LV bus relationships; inspect upstream-relevant LV bus ties or sources without expanding into outgoing LV circuits.
- [ ] Render the connected area views and demonstrate supported controls, source/earth connectivity, affected assets and path tracing.
- [ ] Record incomplete topology, identities, source availability, starting states and interlock conditions as unconfirmed review matters.
- [ ] Update coverage records and validate the area's evidence-linked model with session regression checks.

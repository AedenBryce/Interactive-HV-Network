1. SV-S05 p1 F-G / 1-3 has a dashed linkage/triangle between the transformer feeder's disconnecting and E102 earth arrangement. **Inference:** it is candidate mechanical-interlock evidence. The source does not supply a complete truth table, key identity or installed permitted sequence. Do not silently turn the linkage into an accepted rule or assume no additional restrictions.
2. SV-S06 p1 B-C / 4-6 separately depicts the two RM413A earth locations and Q1 CB. SV-S09 uses other names/symbols. **Unconfirmed:** their full physical mechanism and interlock conditions; no independent switching permission is established.
3. SV-S02's MCS is labelled MAIN CHANGEOVER SWITCH and wired to two generators. It does not show the internal contact-position matrix, neutral switching, overlap/break sequence or a full physical interlock. Those details need confirmation; no common all-terminals-connected state is supported.
4. SV-S10 note3 p1 A / 11-12 explicitly says mechanical interlock of earthing is to be provided to switchyard and power station. It does not establish an installed RM721 key sequence. The already unresolved SW411 control review remains separate from downstream local equipment reviews.
5. No complete administrative switching procedure or complete physical-interlock rule set for these services assets is established by the reviewed pages. Physical and administrative restrictions must remain distinct. The source does not confirm that silence means switching is permitted.
6. CTs, O/C relay, SEPAM T20, REF615, Bender insulation monitors, ELM circuits, metering, 24/48 V control supplies and dashed protection lines are not extra phase supply connections. SV-S05 distinguishes TF412 winding phase conductors from CT and insulation-monitor wiring; SV-S06 distinguishes converter AC inputs from DC and controls.
7. Transformer star neutral, MEN link, tank/screen/earth bars and the RMU earthing contacts are different relationships. SV-S07 p1 B-C shows neutral-to-MEN-to-earth independently of the MCC414 phase bus. Do not infer a permanent phase-earth edge, a universal winding-earth pair or a supply bridge through common physical earth.
8. **Unconfirmed:** source-transfer/backfeed and earth continuity through every transformer/converter. Keep primary and each secondary terminal group separate. Provisional supply-transfer candidates require explicit evidence/status; no real transformer should use generic galvanic all-winding pairs.

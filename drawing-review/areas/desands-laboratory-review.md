**Unconfirmed:** a complete source-transfer/backfeed policy between windings, a transformer phase-earth transfer policy, and installed transformer terminal relationships beyond the drawn supply arrangement. A provisional supply relationship may be recorded with its evidence and unresolved transfer policy, consistent with the owner's permission to build visibly provisional interpretations. It must not be presented as a confirmed installed backfeed or earthing rule.

### LV circuits and converter boundaries

The first LV buses listed above are the agreed endpoints. The VSD/ACS800 feeders, motors, safety-relay circuits and auxiliary UPS/DC/control lines shown below those buses are outgoing LV circuits. Their presence has been reviewed for scope, but they are excluded from the runtime power graph under the agreed boundary. These reviewed Desands supply branches show a final 400 V or 690 V bus before those drive circuits; this review has not identified an intermediate HV converter load requiring an extension past that first LV bus.

D05/D06 continue MC426's existing bus without a new transformer incomer. D08/D09 continue SW427's two buses. D14/D15 continue SW429's two buses. D17 continues SW439's two buses. D19 continues MC430's bus. D22 continues SW443's two buses, including a drive branch labelled future; a future outgoing LV drive does not establish current commissioning of an HV supply branch.

D04 also depicts LV feeds to MC440 and MC443. These are beyond the first LV bus boundary. Do not silently turn an auxiliary LV feed into the primary HV/transformer source of SW440 or SW443.

## Interlocks, protective earth and starting states

D01/D02 show circuit-breaker/earth-switch graphics and dashed control/protection linkages. They do not supply a complete installed physical-interlock condition table or key sequence in the reviewed notes. D04/D10/D18 show incomer control and CT references; D07/D13/D16/D20/D21 show separate D/Y incomer controls. Those references alone do not establish permissive switching rules. Interlock coverage remains **Unconfirmed** for the represented controls; unresolved controls must be disabled under the confirmed project requirement.

D08 (11098-07110-EL-DR-2464, rev 13, page 1, upper-right note 2) expressly describes a key-switch interlock for the **SH131 power isolator**, preventing it being turned off without first operating the key switch; the same note describes an auxiliary contact into SH131's statutory safety-relay circuit to trip the VSD contactor. This is evidence of that drawing's downstream SH131 note, not a confirmed rule for the SW426 HV incomer or SW427 LV bus incomers. SH131 is beyond the agreed LV endpoint. Its installed details remain **Unconfirmed**.

D25-D29 are protective-earth/equipotential-bonding sheets for switchrooms/equipment. They distinguish earth bars, structures, tanks/screens and equipment bonds. The source does not confirm a phase-to-earth switching path merely because the tank, screen, earth bar or neutral is bonded. Do not use a common earth representation as a supply bridge between the sections.

D24 is a physical electrical layout/equipment list, labelled information only/not for construction. Its placement and future spaces do not create electrical graph edges or establish that listed equipment is presently commissioned.

All initial CB positions, earth-switch positions, source availability and owner acceptance remain **Unconfirmed**. The drawn switch pose, an as-built stamp or a revision date has not been accepted as the normal starting configuration.

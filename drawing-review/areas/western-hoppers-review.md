### Transformer and earth relationship boundary

The pages depict separate windings and rated phase routes. **Unconfirmed:** executable bidirectional source transfer/backfeed behaviour and phase earth continuity across each transformer. Candidate source relationships can be prepared as explicitly provisional pairs for owner review. Never use a shared generic galvanic pair merely because two windings belong to one transformer.

WH-S01/S04/S09 separately show DYN11 windings, LV star/neutral earth symbols and an NER/neutral CT on each 6.9 kV transformer. WH-S07 and WH-S13 show protective earth bars, bonded enclosures, transformer neutral/NER paths and LV MEN links. These are not evidence that the phase bus is permanently earthed. Keep phase, neutral, equipment earth and control/trip conductors distinct. A common physical earth system must not become a path bridging independent phase circuits.

- **Hopper8 SS416:** WH-S04 reaches TF416A -> MC416A and TF416 -> MC416 with CB17OP023D-01006-DR-EL-0003/-0002 references. These exact filenames were not found in the supplied inventory. Do not substitute Hopper7's CB17OP023C-0002/-0003 pages or Hopper1's MC417 sheets simply because ratings/layout are similar.

The pages depict sizer motor branches; they do not establish a further LV bus on those routes. Preserve the HV motor interfaces/assemblies and their earthing/interlock evidence; do not invent LV conversion downstream of the HV motor.
- The exact missing filenames above do not prove that no equivalent source exists under another name. The source set has low-text scanned pages; this is not an exhaustive fresh visual search of all 141 originals. No coverage boundary is silently declared disconnected.
